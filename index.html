2300506 Industrial Science

Huaqiao University, International College of New Energy · with Chulalongkorn University · Semester 2, 2026–2027

Quantitative tools for industrial decisions, taught through one case: CellCo, a battery cell maker. Every deck has English narration and English captions that you can turn on or off, interactive slides, and an in-class activity.

Before Block 1: prepare your laptop with this one-page guide and run check_setup.py.
How to use the slides: the buttons at the bottom left turn the narration, captions and auto-advance on or off. Click a slide or press → to go forward, ← to go back. If the site is slow, download the offline copy of a session, unzip it, and open the session file (for example s01.html).
