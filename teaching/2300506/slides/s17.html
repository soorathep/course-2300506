Question for today: CellCo’s coating is in control. Is it good enough for a customer who demands Cpk 1.33, and what would it cost to get there?

When readings remember the past

A beta gauge scans the web every second. Consecutive readings are close because the oven, slurry and foil change slowly: the data are autocorrelated.

Lag-1 autocorrelation for CellCo’s continuous readings: 0.7.

Standard charts assume independent points. With autocorrelation, the moving range is small, so I-MR limits come out too narrow: about ±1.64 process standard deviations instead of ±3.

About 10% of in-control points then fall outside the limits. Operators learn to ignore the alarms, and then miss the real ones.

Remedy. Fit a time-series model, here AR(1): xt = μ + φ(xt−1 − μ) + et. Chart the residuals et, which are independent if the model fits.

See the false alarms

The process is in control throughout. Every red point is a false alarm.

Charts for counts

p chart: fraction defective. Each lot of n = 500 cells; average defective fraction p̄ = 3.5%.

p̄ ± 3√(p̄(1 − p̄)/n) = 3.5% ± 2.47%

Limits 1.03% to 5.97%. If lot sizes vary, the limits vary with n.

c chart: defects per unit. Pinholes counted on each 100 m² of electrode; average c̄ = 4.

c̄ ± 3√c̄ = 4 ± 6

Limits 0 to 10. A roll with 11 or more pinholes signals.

Attribute charts need far more data than variables charts to see the same shift. Measure the mass loading itself where you can; count defects where you cannot.

Break · 10 minutes

After the break: the process is in control. Is it capable?

Process capability

In control means stable and predictable. Capable means the predictable output fits the specification. A process can be one without the other.

Cp = (USL − LSL) / 6σ = 1.2 / (6 × 0.172) = 1.16

Cpk = min(USL − μ, μ − LSL) / 3σ = 0.55 / 0.516 = 1.07

Out of specification: about 770 ppm, almost all above 20.6.

Cpk against Ppk. Cpk uses the within-subgroup σ (R̄/d2); Ppk uses the overall σ of all readings, which also includes drift between subgroups. An overall σ of 0.19 would give Ppk = 0.96.

Normality. ppm figures assume a normal distribution. Check with a histogram and a normal probability plot; tails matter most.

Capability means nothing for a process out of control: its future output cannot be predicted.

Explore capability

Re-centering alone cuts out-of-specification output by 37%, to about 485 ppm, at no change in variation. Cpk 1.33 also needs σ to fall 13%, to 0.150.

Activity · Specification negotiation

Paired groups · 25 minutes · agreed plan with ppm and cost

One group is CellCo (supplier); the other is a storage integrator (customer) who requires Cpk 1.33. Agree a plan, with who pays what.

Supplier’s options

  • Re-center the die: one-time CNY 0.1 million.
  • New slot die with closed-loop loading control, σ to 0.150: one-time CNY 1.2 million.

Customer’s costs

Each 100 ppm out of specification costs the customer CNY 0.15 million a year in sorting and returns.

Each side may concede only with a number that supports it.

What we learned

Autocorrelated data make standard limits too narrow: with φ = 0.7, about 10% false alarms. Chart model residuals instead.

p and c charts monitor counts, but need more data than charts of measurements.

In control is not capable. CellCo is at Cpk 1.07 and 770 ppm; re-centering plus a 13% cut in σ reaches 1.33 and 63 ppm.
