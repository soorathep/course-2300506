The PERT answer

With expected durations, the critical path is A-B-C-F-H, 20.67 weeks. A-B-E-G-H follows at 20.17 weeks.

PERT adds the variances along the critical path: σ² = 0.44 + 0.69 + 1.78 + 0.11 + 0.25 = 3.28, so σ = 1.81 weeks.

P(finish ≤ 22) = Φ((22 − 20.67)/1.81) = Φ(0.74) = 0.77.

PERT looks only at one path. But the second path is just 0.5 week shorter. In the futures where E or G runs late, it decides the finish date, and PERT ignores those futures.

Check PERT by simulation

The simulation draws every duration from a beta-PERT distribution with the same a, m and b, and takes the longest path in each run.

The simulated mean finish is 21.1 weeks, not 20.7. A-B-E-G-H turns out to be the longest path in 41% of runs. Where paths merge, the finish date is the maximum of several uncertain paths, and that maximum is later than any one path alone.

For a 90% on-time promise, PERT suggests 23.0 weeks; the simulation says 23.4.

Rules of thumb. Crash only critical activities. Prefer the cheapest per week. An activity shared by both long paths (A, B or H) shortens both at once.

As an LP. Choose crash weeks xk to minimize Σ ck xk, with every path’s expected length minus its crashing at most 19, and 0 ≤ xk ≤ maxk. Block 2 solves LPs like this in Solver.

Activity · Crash auction

Groups of 4 · 30 minutes · price list and the planner

Reach 19 expected weeks at the lowest crash cost. The cheapest valid plan wins.

  1. Plan on paper first, using the rules of thumb. Write your plan and its cost.
  2. Check it on the planner. Did any crash week buy nothing? Why?
  3. If every week saved also saves CNY 0.30 million of site overhead, how far should CellCo crash?
  4. Each group posts its plan and cost. Then we reveal the LP optimum.

B is shared by both long paths and is the cheapest critical activity, so it goes first. Crashing D buys nothing: D is not critical.

With overhead of CNY 0.30 million per week, crashing pays while the marginal cost is below 0.30: B (0.25) pays, C (0.30) breaks even, A (0.40) does not. So crash B fully.

These are expected durations. Crashing to 19 expected weeks still leaves a real chance of finishing later.

What we learned

PERT turns three estimates into an expected duration and a variance. For CellCo it gives a 77% chance of finishing in 22 weeks.

Simulation of all paths gives 71% and a mean of 21.1 weeks. Merging paths make PERT optimistic.

Crashing is an LP. Finishing in 19 expected weeks costs at least CNY 0.425 million, by crashing B, which both long paths share, and a little of C.
