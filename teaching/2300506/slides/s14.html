Question for today: CellCo must fix next week’s energy-storage output before it knows demand. Should it plan for average demand?

Produce first, learn demand later

Weekly energy-storage demand is uncertain:

Demand, MWh204080Mean
Probability0.30.50.242

Each MWh sold earns CNY 30,000 over its cost. Each MWh made but not sold loses its cost of CNY 30,000.

Two-stage stochastic LP

Stage 1, now: choose output x.

Stage 2, after demand dk is known: sell sk ≤ x and sk ≤ dk.

max Σk pk (60sk − 30x)

One copy of the second-stage variables per scenario. With many products and scenarios, this is an ordinary LP, only larger.

Explore the plan

The curve is steeper to the right of the best output than to the left: overproducing by 10 MWh costs more than underproducing by 10.

Robust planning

A robust plan assumes only an interval, demand between 20 and 80, and maximizes the worst-case profit:

max over x of the minimum over d in [20, 80] of 60 min(x, d) − 30x

The answer is x = 20: CNY 600,000 a week, guaranteed.

The guarantee costs 840 − 600 = CNY 240,000 a week in expected profit, the price of robustness.

Robust planning suits cases where probabilities are unknown or a bad outcome is unacceptable, such as a safety stock for a hospital customer. Narrower intervals, or a limit on how many uncertain values can be at their worst together, reduce the price.

Activity · Commit before the shock

Groups of 4 · 45 minutes · scorecard

Six rounds, one week each. Commit your output x (a multiple of 5, at most 80 MWh) on the scorecard. Then a demand card and a shock card are turned.

Demand deck: three 20s, five 40s, two 80s.

Shock deck: four “no shock”, two “lithium carbonate +40%” (cost per MWh 36 instead of 30), two “energy-storage demand −20%”, two “coating line outage” (at most 30 MWh made this week).

Score = 60 × MWh sold − cost × MWh made, in CNY thousand.

After round 6, add a second column: what output 40 would have scored on the same cards. Explain the difference.

With the shocks included, the stochastic plan is still 40 MWh, worth CNY 706,000 per round in expectation. Perfect foresight would be worth 1,069.

Why 40 survives the shocks. Only the demand −20% card favors less (32). The lithium card raises cost but leaves 40 best; the outage caps output whatever you commit.

Luck versus skill. Six rounds are too few to judge a plan by its score. Comparing with output 40 on the same cards removes the luck of the draw.

What we learned

When output is fixed before demand is known, plan with the scenarios, not with the mean. For CellCo, 40 MWh beats the mean of 42 by CNY 36,000 a week.

EVPI bounds the value of forecasting: CNY 420,000 a week here. A robust plan buys a guarantee at a price, CNY 240,000 a week here.

Judge a plan by its expected result on the same scenarios, not by one lucky or unlucky outcome.
