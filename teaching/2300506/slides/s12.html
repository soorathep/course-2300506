Simplex: walking along corners

The simplex method starts at a corner and moves along an edge to a better neighbor until no neighbor is better.

  1. Start at (0, 0). P has the larger margin, so increase P until power-cell demand stops it at 60.
  2. At (0, 60), increasing E still pays. Coating stops it at E = 30.
  3. At (30, 60), no edge improves contribution: optimal after two steps.

Real LPs have thousands of corners; simplex visits only a few. Interior-point methods, used by large solvers, cut through the middle instead.

Setting up Solver

Layout in Excel. Decision cells for E and P; objective =SUMPRODUCT(margins, decisions); one row per constraint with hours used next to hours available.

Solver dialog. Maximize the objective by changing the decision cells, subject to used ≤ available, with variables non-negative. Method: Simplex LP. Ask for the Sensitivity report.

Keep data, formulas and decisions in separate cells. A model with numbers typed inside formulas cannot be audited or re-run with new data.

WPS Office has a compatible solver if Excel is not available. Python users can reproduce the model in PuLP.

How to read each line

Shadow price. Change in the optimal objective per unit increase in a right-hand side, holding all other data fixed.

Allowable increase and decrease of a right-hand side: the range over which the shadow price stays valid. Coating: 180 to 300 hours.

Reduced cost of a variable: how much its margin must improve before it enters the plan. Zero for products already made.

Allowable range of a coefficient: the margins over which the current plan stays optimal. E: 0 to 32. P: 45 or more.

Inside a range, the plan’s mix of binding constraints stays the same, so the answer moves in a straight line. At the edge, a new constraint binds and the price changes.

Where shadow prices come from

Each straight piece is one allowable range. The slope of the piece is the shadow price; where the slope changes, a different constraint has started to bind.

What we learned

Simplex walks from corner to better corner. Solver’s sensitivity report comes free with the solution.

A shadow price holds only inside its range: CNY 15,000 per coating hour from 180 to 300 hours, then zero.

Most what-if questions are answered by one report line, if the change is single and inside the range.
