Variables are fractions a, b, c ≥ 0 with a + b + c = 1. Capacity and moisture blend linearly by mass.

Optimal blend: 50% A, 50% C, CNY 44,000 per tonne. Capacity is exactly 156 (binding); moisture is 425 ppm (slack).

B, the “middle” lot, is not used: mixing the best and the cheapest lots beats it on both cost and capacity.

The capacity shadow price is CNY 2,000 per tonne per mAh/g: tightening the spec to 157 raises the cost to 46.

Crashing returns as an LP

From Session 8: choose crash weeks xk for each activity.

min 0.40xA + 0.25xB + 0.30xC + … + 0.60xH

subject to, for each path, its expected length minus its crash weeks ≤ 19, for example

20.67 − xA − xB − xC − xF − xH ≤ 19

and 0 ≤ xk ≤ maximum crash.

Solver returns xB = 1.5, xC = 0.17, cost CNY 0.425 million, as found in the crash auction.

The shadow price of the 19-week target is 0.30: each further week costs CNY 300,000 until 18.67 weeks, where the second path also binds and A must be crashed.

Network problems are LPs with special structure. Shortest path, maximal flow, transportation and crashing can all be written this way.

A 13-week plan with a second shift

Demand rises to 110 MWh in week 8. One shift makes 80 MWh a week. A second shift adds 40 MWh but costs CNY 250,000 for each week it runs. Holding stock costs CNY 3,000 per MWh per week. Stock starts at 10 and must end at 10 or more.

MILP. For weeks t = 1 to 13, with yt ∈ {0, 1}:

min Σ (250yt + 3It)

It = It−1 + xt − dt

xt ≤ 80 + 40yt

The last constraint links the binary to capacity: without it, the model would produce 120 MWh without ever paying for the shift.

The relaxation is a lower bound, not a plan: nobody can run 0.38 of a shift.

Given the shift weeks, production follows “make as late as capacity allows”, which minimizes stock. Try to beat 1,011.

What we learned

Transportation, blending and crashing are LPs. Greedy choices, such as the cheapest route or the middle-grade lot, are often not optimal.

Binary variables model yes-or-no decisions. Each must be linked to the capacity or cost it controls.

For CellCo, three well-placed shift weeks cost CNY 1,011 thousand, against 1,798 for a simple rule.
