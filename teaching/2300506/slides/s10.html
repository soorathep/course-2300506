Activity · Peer review and idea card

Groups, then pairs · 25 minutes · review checklist and idea card

Part 1 (15 minutes). Swap your draft network plan with another group and review it against the checklist.

  1. Every activity and precedence is listed.
  2. Durations have three estimates and a source.
  3. The critical path and slack are correct.
  1. The on-time probability comes from simulation, not PERT alone.
  2. The crash decision states its cost.
  3. Shared crews or equipment are checked.

Part 2 (10 minutes). Write a three-sentence idea card for your research note: the decision, the method, the data. Swap with a partner and ask one question about their card.
